Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham

Mata Amritanandamayi (Amma) emphasizes love,
compassion, and self-realization through simple, profound wisdom.
Her teachings often highlight that true happiness comes from selfless service,
seeing the divine in everyone, and maintaining inner peace regardless of external circumstances,
with the core message that "love is the only medicine that can heal the wounds of the world".
