About Korean Fried Chicken

Korean Fried Chicken is a popular South Korean dish known for its extra crispy texture and flavorful sauces. Unlike regular fried chicken, it is often double-fried to make it crunchier. It is usually coated in a sweet and spicy sauce made with gochujang (Korean chili paste). This dish is enjoyed as a snack or meal and is loved all around the world.
