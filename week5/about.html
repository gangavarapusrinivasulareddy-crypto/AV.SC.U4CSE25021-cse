Korean Fried Chicken Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 500g chicken wings
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1/2 cup cornflour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp pepper
  • Oil for frying

Instructions:

  1. Coat chicken with flour mixture.
  2. Deep fry until golden and crispy.
  3. Prepare spicy sauce with gochujang and honey.
  4. Toss fried chicken in sauce.
  5. Serve hot and enjoy!
